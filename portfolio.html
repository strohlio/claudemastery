Selected work,
preserved in ice.

01 — Glacier OS

Operating systems for cold climates.

A low-power runtime for autonomous sensors deployed across Svalbard. Survives -40°C, runs for nine months on a single charge, and uplinks only when the aurora is quiet.

02 — Permafrost Index

A real-time map of thaw.

Satellite-fed dashboard tracking ground temperature across 14,000 monitoring stations. Used by three national meteorological services to issue infrastructure warnings.

03 — Crystal Lattice

Generative crystallography.

A research tool that grows synthetic crystal structures from a single seed parameter. Outputs printable lattice geometry used in lightweight aerospace alloys.

04 — Polar Archive

An archive that doesn't melt.

Cold-storage protocol for cultural records. Distributes 1PB of high-resolution scans across mirrored bunkers above the Arctic Circle, with verification every solstice.
